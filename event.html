Fall Campus Festival

Category: Social

October 3, 2026 at 4:00 PM

Location: University Quad

The Fall Campus Festival brings students together for an afternoon of food, music, games, student organization booths, and campus activities.

Event Details

The Fall Campus Festival is designed to help students connect with campus organizations, enjoy live entertainment, and participate in activities throughout the afternoon.

What to Expect

  • Live music and student performances
  • Food trucks and campus dining vendors
  • Games and activity stations
  • Student organization information booths

Schedule

  • 4:00 PM - Festival opens
  • 4:30 PM - Student performances begin
  • 6:00 PM - Campus games and activities
  • 8:00 PM - Closing performance

Accessibility and Participation

The event is open to all students. Accessible seating and pathways will be available throughout the event area. Students who need additional accommodations may contact the Office of Student Engagement.

Event Information

Date: October 3, 2026

Time: 4:00 PM - 8:30 PM

Location: University Quad

Organizer: Office of Student Engagement

Admission: Free for students
